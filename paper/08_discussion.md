# Discussion

Explicit structural representations and configurable objectives make a document-processing prototype inspectable. That is a design property; its usefulness to authors remains a question for evaluation. An optimized numerical reward can favor undesirable edits, so factual preservation, task success and human understanding need independent checks.

Belief distributions provide a vocabulary for uncertainty, but normalization alone does not establish empirical calibration or semantic truth. Similarly, a Markov or policy interface does not confer an optimality guarantee without a verified model, solver and assumptions. Comparisons with language models require identified baselines and matched data and execution conditions.

The missing corpus, checkpoint, participant and statistical records limit this revision's conclusions. Withdrawing unsupported values is preferable to deriving replacement numbers from examples or test counts. The protocol defines what future evidence must contain; it is not a claim that the studies have occurred. Historical deposits remain a separate publication epoch.
